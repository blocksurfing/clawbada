# Review of the contract work since the restart (2026-10-01 → 2026-10-02), run with Fable 5.1

**Scope.** Everything merged to `main` from `b9f52b8` (after PR #173) to `8c945c5`: PR #174 (the Safe starts each season), PR #175 (BattleArena rewritten — no player disputes, review window, GUARDIAN freeze, 72 h long-stop with burn + refund reserve, consent and team commit inside `deposit`, `forfeiter`, draw fee), PR #176 (D-19 hourly glide + per-epoch spend ceiling + dust hold), and everything they touched off-chain (engine watchdog, settle job, reveal watcher, API, indexer, deploy scripts, runbooks, docs). About 4,400 lines of contract and test change, plus the off-chain side. Nothing is deployed anywhere yet, so every contract change is still free.

**Method.** A full read of `BattleArena.sol` and the D-19 diff by the lead, six independent read-only review lenses (funds + state machine; roles, timing, griefing; the off-chain trust path; D-19 with fresh eyes; test adequacy; deploy scripts + docs), then verification by the lead: five forge proofs-of-concept on the real contracts and a demand-shape probe of the glide controller (both kept in the session scratchpad, not committed — they become regression tests with the fixes). Line numbers are for the current files.

## Verdict, in plain terms

**The money is safe.** Nobody but the two players and the Treasury's 10 % can ever receive a battle's escrow; every path conserves `balance == escrow + refundReserve`; every phase has a permissionless way out; the windows do not overlap or leave gaps; the burn-and-repay long-stop does what the decision said. No High finding. The hot keys cannot enrich themselves. The glide arithmetic is right and the mutation checks hold.

**Four things are not what we decided, or were never modelled, and all are cheap now and impossible after launch:**

1. **The 5 % anti-grief deposit we decided to "make real" still has a free way around it.** A player can deposit, then make their own team unrevealable (disband it, send it mining, let its Power change) — the reveal fails, nobody can be blamed, both get a full refund. Repeatable, costs gas only. (M1)
2. **Honest battles will be frozen by our own watchdog** when a bleeding lobster dies at the start of a timed-out turn — normal play, no attacker. The Safe then has to hand-resolve; if it doesn't within 72 h the winner loses the pot. (M2)
3. **Every season would restart at the 1,250 launch rate**, so season starts become hours-long "mine is full" races, and from Season 5 the ceiling's one-expedition floor is larger than the "twice the fair share" guarantee. The rollover plan the engine prepares for the Safe proposes 1,250 every time. (M5)
4. **The glide simulation only ran smooth hourly demand.** The hourly controller reads "the last hour that had demand" as the demand of every hour; with expeditions lasting exactly 4 epochs, bunched demand (cron-driven agents, a whale alternating idle/burst, a daily rhythm) makes it under- or over-pay cohorts by time of day. The budget is never at risk (the ceiling), fairness is. A one-line estimator change fixes the clear cases; it needs choosing with the model before Sepolia. (M6)

Then a set of first-deploy items that are operational rather than code: guardian failures are not alarmed until the window has closed; the watchdog only sees what the indexer mirrors; the faucet and Season 1 clocks start at `Deploy`/`Configure`, before the Safe steps that open the game; `startSeason` accepts any number and a typo cannot be amended for 60 days; there is no Sepolia runbook. And two behaviours with no test at all (a player committing someone else's team; the Safe clearing a proposed forfeit).

## Findings

Severity: **High** = funds or liveness at risk · **Medium** = a decided behaviour not delivered, a bug in normal play, or a first-deploy hazard · **Low** = hardening, griefing without loss, missing tests · **Info** = wording or dead code.

### A. BattleArena.sol

**M1 (Medium) — An openable commit to an unplayable team is a free, no-fault cancel.** `openOwnCommit` (`contracts/BattleArena.sol:422-434`) proves only the hash; fault at the reveal lapse is `accused && !opened` (`:802-803`); `_validateTeamForBattle` (`:673-687`) runs only inside `revealTeams`. A team is not locked until the reveal, so between deposit and reveal its owner can still disband it (`TeamManager.disbandTeam` refuses only `active` teams), send it mining (`MiningPool.startExpedition` likewise), commit it in two battles, or let its Power move. The reveal then reverts `TeamNotOwned` / `TeamAlreadyInBattle` / `TeamPowerChanged`, the resolver has no on-chain way to attribute it, and an accusation is cleared by opening the hash. **Proved on the real contracts** (three PoCs): disband after deposit → `MutualTimeout`, both refunded in full; mining the committed team in the same block → same; a Power change → same. No money moves and no information leaks (F5-01 holds), so it is zero-EV grief — but it is exactly the class the owner decided should cost 5 %, and the contract comment at `:44-46` and the spec say it does. No test exercises a reveal failing on team validity. *Fix: D-A.*

**L1 (Low) — Payouts whose review window has closed have no escape if the Treasury de-authorises the arena.** `finalizeBattle` / `handleTimeout` → `_executePayout` → `treasury.processFee` (`:704-705, :718-719`); `freeze` is refused after the window (`:497`), so the Safe cannot route the battle to `resolveFrozen(refundBoth)` / `expireFrozen`, which do not touch the Treasury. **Proved**: after `setAuthorized(arena, false)` a closed review reverts on finalize, timeout and freeze until re-authorised. Safe-only, recoverable. *Fix: a runbook rule (never de-authorise with battles live) and a `VerifyDeployment` assertion; or let the Safe freeze without the window bound.*

**L2 (Low) — `settle` can apply 100 damage per lobster; a rogue result's repair cost has no on-chain remedy.** `_applyDamage` clamps at 100 (`:758-759`); the spec's maximum is 40. A stolen resolver key can bar every lobster in every live battle (≥ 80) and charge up to 12,000 CLAW of repairs per Apex team; the decision says the treasury makes players whole by hand. *Fix: D-F.*

**L3 (Low) — No server process calls `handleTimeout` for the Deposit / TeamReveal / Active phases.** The finalize watcher sweeps only `finalizeBattle` and `expireFrozen`; the D-14 slash and every pre-settle refund wait for the victim's own transaction (web shows a button; agents are on their own). *Fix: add the three phases to the watcher's permissionless sweep.*

**I1 (Info) — `CancelReason.MutualTimeout` is also emitted when both sides are slashed** (`:804-806`; pinned by `test_bothAccusedNeitherOpenedBothSlashed`). Consumers must key off `AntiGriefSlashed`. *Add `ForfeitBoth` or document.*

**I2 (Info) — `BattleSettled(id, 0, 0, 0)` is identical for refund-both and expiry**; a draw carries the fee. Consumers must use `FrozenResolved` / `FrozenExpired`.

**I3 (Info, accepted design, wording) — The resolver hot key can take 5 % from any depositor who does not answer an accusation within two minutes.** **Proved**: accusation + no `openOwnCommit` → 2,500 back, 125 to the fee split. The NatSpec "a false report cannot cost an honest player anything" (`:403`) holds only for a player watching the chain; the slash goes to the Treasury (85 % burn / 15 % dev), so the key gains nothing — grief only. An accusation also buys a slow resolver +2 min on the 20 s reveal window. *Qualify the NatSpec; the agent kit must handle `RevealFailureAccused` (see C-L5).*

**I4 (Info) — "The guardian moves no money" is only literally true**: a freeze the Safe never resolves ends in refund-both and a 2 × stake burn from the reserve (`:737-740`), and a forfeiter escapes the slash. The runbook states the blast radius; CLAUDE.md's sentence should match.

**I5 (Info) — `emergencyWithdraw` is dead code** (`:612-619`, 24 h after `lastProgressAt`; `handleTimeout` cancels the same battle at 3 h). **I6** — `battleVRF` stored, never used. **I7** — `_cancelWithSlash` would slash a side that never deposited if a future path asked it to (`:783`; unreachable today). **I8** — direct token transfers to the arena are unrecoverable (no sweep). **I9** — after 72 h `expireFrozen` and a late `resolveFrozen` race; whichever lands first wins. **I10 (HARDEN-1, known)** — the commit hash has no chain-id / contract domain separator (`:365, 368, 430`); no exploit found.

**D1 (decision) — Stake brackets are constructor-fixed** (`:257-259`, no setter) while the spec and gitbook say they re-peg per season as multiples of the launch reward. With the glide at ~49 CLAW per Base expedition at 20,000 teams, a Low stake of 2,500 is 51 expeditions' worth; the breakeven economics assumed proportionality. *Fix: D-E.*

### B. MiningPool.sol (D-19) and the economics

**M5 (Medium) — Every season restarts at the launch rate; from S5 the floor exceeds the 2× guarantee.** `startSeason` sets `baseReward = launchBaseReward = parameter` (`contracts/MiningPool.sol:235-245`); the ceiling's floor is one Apex expedition at +50 % of that rate (`:550-551`); the engine's rollover plan proposes the current season's launch reward — 1,250 forever (`apps/engine/src/seasons/manager.ts:144-149`). Fair hourly share vs floor at 1,250: S1 489,583 / 46,875 (floor is 0.19× of the 2× cap, so the fair-share rule holds); S4 61,198 / 46,875 (0.77×); **S5 30,599 / 46,875 (1.5× — the floor IS the cap)**; S7 9,792 / 46,875 (4.8×); S8 ≤ 5,514 / 46,875 (8.5×). From S5 the "no hour mints more than twice its share" backstop is void (one +50 % Apex per hour is still a hard bound, so the budget cannot drain). And at every season start the rate is 11–27× the sustainable one, so the first hours are a first-come race with most starts refused (≈ 7 h to walk down at −30 %/h; ~20 h in S5). *Fix: D-B.*

**M6 (Medium) — The demand estimator is phase-sensitive; the simulation only ran smooth demand.** `_repegIfNeeded` keeps `trailingWeightServed` from the last epoch that had demand (`:573`). A 4 h expedition is exactly four hourly epochs, so a population that starts on the same hour is quiet for three epochs and the controller reads the burst as every hour's demand. Probe on the real controller arithmetic (20,000 Base teams, S1 budget, refused starts retry next hour):

| Demand shape | As deployed: reward d1 / d30 / d60 | Last-4-epoch estimate: d1 / d30 / d60 |
|---|---|---|
| smooth (ideal 49) | 48.6 / 48.6 / 48.6 | 48.5 / 48.5 / 48.5 |
| 50 % phase-locked on one hour in four | 41.8 / 41.3 / 38.8 | 48.5 / 48.5 / 46.0 |
| 100 % phase-locked | 12.2 / 20.3 / 1,250 — **18.5 % unspent**, dumped on the last day | 48.5 / 48.5 / 34.4 |
| whale 40 %: idle 3 h, burst on the 4th | 72.4 / 69.0 / 32.5 (early over-pay, late under-pay) | 48.5 / 48.5 / 65.6 |
| daily rhythm: 60 % of starts in 8 h | oscillates ±30 % within each day, cap binds every peak hour (189 h) | same; a 4-epoch window does not cover a 24 h rhythm |

The ceiling disperses bunched demand (refused starts move to the next hour), so fully locked phases are unlikely at scale, but partial clustering is plausible and nothing modelled it; the daily-rhythm case needs a per-cohort fairness metric rather than rate samples. The related claim from the daily-epoch analysis — "a whale alternating idle/burst strictly loses" — needs re-deriving hourly. *Fix: D-C.*

**M7 (Medium) — `startSeason` accepts any numbers and a wrong season cannot be amended for 60 days; `setBaseReward` has no bound.** `startSeason` checks only non-zero plus the allocation clamp (`:217-227`) and `SeasonStillActive` blocks a corrective call (`:231`); there is no `endSeason`. An emission typed without 18 decimals yields a season whose budget is below one expedition: `SeasonBudgetExhausted` for 60 days. An override above the budget left makes the hold permanent (`:596`) and jumps repair prices; an override of N × launch sets the floor to 37.5 × N × launch for up to an hour. *Fix: D-D, plus a mandatory `cast calldata-decode` step in the rollover runbook.*

**L4 (Low) — `epochBudget()` keeps answering after the season ends** (`:503-513`: `epochsLeft = 1`, `cap = 2 × left`), and `/api/game/mining/start` has no season-active check, so the API says "fits" and the transaction reverts `SeasonNotActive`. The view's floor also uses the pre-re-peg rate on the first touch of an hour (up to 30 % off when the floor binds). *Fix: return zeros after the season; API checks `now < start + 60 d`; expose "quote may move".*

**L5 (Low) — The API quote ignores the boost and a pending re-peg** (`apps/api/src/routes/game/mining.ts:139-152`): a +50 % Apex team is told "fits" at 31,250 and reverts at 46,875; an hour boundary makes the quoted reward 30 % off. *Fix: `simulateContract` for the exact reward and revert, or include `teamBoostBps`.*

**I11 (Info) — Observability.** The ceiling binding is invisible to the indexer (a revert); the two holds (`:578, :596`) are silent; nothing calls `repeg()` on a schedule, so repair prices lag the glide in quiet hours. *Suggest an `EpochRolled(season, epoch, trailing, cap)` event and an hourly `repeg()` from the engine.* **I12** — stale "daily" wording at `MiningPool.sol:15, :567`, `manager.ts:145`; the stale `getSeasonEmission` schedule in `packages/game-logic/src/constants.ts:157-171` has no callers but is still exported.

### C. The off-chain trust path

**M2 (Medium, bug in normal play) — An honest result is frozen when a bleeding lobster dies at the start of a timed-out turn.** `applyTurn` logs `action: 'skip'` when the actor died to bleed before acting (`packages/game-logic/src/v3/turn.ts:187-193, 248`); the session's timeout path then marks that entry `timeout = true` (`v3/session.ts:85-88`); replay rejects any timed-out entry that is not a plain Defend (`v3/replay.ts:80-83`: "a timed-out turn must be a plain Defend"); the watchdog turns the failed replay into `freeze()` (`apps/engine/src/combat/finalize-watcher.ts:88, 233-245`). The documented hand-check (`v3.verifyLog`) fails the same way, so the Safe cannot confirm the result by the written method; after 72 h `expireFrozen` burns 2 × stake and refunds both — the honest winner loses the pot. The existing replay tests forfeit after three timeouts before a bleed can kill, so this is uncovered. *Fix: `ForfeitAudit.checkTurn` accepts `timeout` on a `skip` entry from a non-stunned actor and still counts the streak (no rules-version bump); a test with a bleeding lobster at ≤ bleed HP and a `{type:'timeout'}` event → `reproduceSession(...).ok`. Also treat a thrown error in `review()` as "not clean" rather than a retried tick (`finalize-watcher.ts:202-206`).*

**M3 (Medium) — Guardian freeze failures are not alarmed until the window has closed; no boot preflight.** `freeze()` rethrows every non-benign error (`finalize-watcher.ts:255-283, :307-343`); a key without `GUARDIAN_ROLE`, an empty gas wallet or an RPC error is logged as the generic `watchdog step failed` every tick, and the first freeze-specific alarm (`battle_freeze_missed`, `:234-241`) fires after `payoutDeadline`, when `finalizeBattle` is already permissionless. Mainnet checks only that the key is set (`apps/engine/src/env-check.ts:35`), not that it holds the role or has ETH. A `GUARDIAN_ADDRESS` set at `Configure` with no matching `GUARDIAN_PRIVATE_KEY` on the engine (testnet fallback to the operator key, `packages/chain/src/client.ts:98-111`) fails exactly this way. *Fix: catch in `freeze()` and log `battle_freeze_failed` (fatal) with the reason on every attempt; boot preflight `hasRole(GUARDIAN_ROLE, guardian)` + minimum balance, re-checked periodically; alert on it.*

**M4 (Medium) — Watchdog coverage depends on indexer and engine liveness, with no second source or heartbeat.** The watchdog's only input is the DB mirror (`finalize-watcher.ts:192-195`), written by the indexer's `BattleProposed` handler; the indexer's live loop has no `onError` and no lag metric (`apps/indexer/src/lib/event-processor.ts:86-97`). Indexer or engine down for longer than the 5-minute Low window and a rogue result pays out through the permissionless `finalizeBattle`; the first signal is retrospective. *Fix: read `BattleProposed` logs from the RPC for the last N blocks as a second source; a watchdog heartbeat with an external alert on silence; indexer `onError` + lag alarm; optionally run the (idempotent) freeze path in a second process.*

**C-L1 (Low) — Sequential, unordered tick with inline receipt waits**: a freeze can queue behind many finalizes (`finalize-watcher.ts:198-209, :324-325`). *Order by `payoutDeadline`; judge/freeze pass before finalize pass; do not await receipts inline.* **C-L2** — `finalizeBattle` / `expireFrozen` failures have no distinct alarm; paging stops past the long-stop (`:291-303`). **C-L3** — the season gap is invisible to players (`/start` has no pre-check, no `seasonEndsAt` anywhere) and the season manager is blind without a `seasons` row (`manager.ts:171-172`; an indexer cold-started without `INDEXER_START_BLOCK` never sees `SeasonStarted`). **C-L4** — the 5 s resume floor after a restart (`apps/api/src/lib/battle-session/manager.ts:439-441`) can turn three restarts into an honest player's forfeit. **C-L5** — the agent kit mints a fresh salt per `deposit()` call (`scripts/e2e/lib/agent.ts:163`): a retry after a landed deposit overwrites the server's salt with one that does not open → immediate accusation, and nothing pushes `RevealFailureAccused` to the player; the `/deposit` comment "a stale or wrong one costs nothing" predates D-14. The API's D-17 refusal to store a salt when no queued-team row exists (`battle-writes.ts:236-247`) looks to the watcher like "no salt" and gets an honest player accused at +12 s. *Persist the salt per battle; record refused reveal attempts as non-accusable; emit a WS event on accusation; `/deposit` must not overwrite a stored salt once the deposit landed unless the new one opens the commit.* **C-L6** — the web routes an accused user to the gas path with a per-tab salt; a free re-POST of `/reveal-team` would clear most accusations (`apps/web/src/components/game/battle-moves.tsx:103-104, 455-480`).

**C-Info.** `readOpenedCommit` scans 5,000 blocks of logs (`reveal-watcher.ts:117-126`) — RPC tiers that cap the range make an opened commit unusable; `AntiGriefSlashed` / `RevealFailureAccused` are not persisted by the indexer; `expireFrozen` voids boost participation even for a legitimately played battle that was falsely frozen (interacts with M2); the e2e drill covers `session_still_active` only, not `no_session`, `result_mismatch`, a restart mid-review, or the accusation path; the `depositConsentMismatch` check uses the game-logic `STAKE_BRACKETS` constant, so a bracket change fails closed.

### E. Tests
